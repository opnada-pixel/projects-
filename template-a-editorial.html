A 315-room hotel in central Abu Dhabi, finished in stone from the lobby floor to the rooftop pool.

Completed in September 2016, the hotel combines business and leisure: meeting spaces, restaurants, a spa, a fitness centre and a rooftop pool.

El Alfy Saraya supplied and installed the stone throughout, so the interiors and the pool deck read as one material.

01

Cladding, bathrooms and floors

Marble wall cladding in corridors, lounges and bathrooms, custom marble vanities, and bright marble floors through the lobby and public areas.

02

Rooftop pool finishes

Polished beige marble tiles around the rooftop pool, matched to the interior palette.
